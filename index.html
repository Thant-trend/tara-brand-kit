Safe areas
Top inset 59px to clear the Dynamic Island. Bottom inset 34px for the home indicator. Tab bar floats 26px above it.

      
Touch
Minimum target 44×44. Buttons are 48px tall with 16px radius. Gutter between controls is at least 8px.

      
Motion
Taps use 120–160ms ease-out. The ticket glow drifts over 7s and stops under reduced-motion.

      
Accessibility
Ink text on every brand surface. Focus ring 2px aqua with a 2px offset. Never signal state with colour alone.
